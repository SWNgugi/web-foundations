About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos the moment they come to mind. It keeps everything in one short list so you can find and review your notes at a glance.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" box.
  3. Click "Add Note" to save it to your list.
  4. Review your notes under "Your notes".

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See a live count of your notes
  • Simple, distraction-free layout
  • Works in any modern browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you are typing
